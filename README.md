IFC BIZGROWTH

Building Digital Bridges. Growing African Businesses.

IFC BIZGROWTH is an African business discovery, digital visibility, and growth platform designed to help businesses showcase their products and services, establish an online presence, reach potential customers, and access digital advertising and marketing opportunities.

The platform connects businesses with customers through an online business directory and public business profiles. It provides businesses with digital tools to present their offerings, manage their online presence, promote their businesses, and understand how people interact with their profiles.

IFC BIZGROWTH operates under IFC BRIDGE LAB, a technology and innovation company focused on building digital products, platforms, and solutions that connect people, businesses, and opportunities.

Our long-term vision is to support business growth and digital participation across all 54 African countries.

About the Project

IFC BIZGROWTH is being developed to make it easier for businesses to become discoverable online and connect with potential customers.

Many small and medium-sized businesses struggle to establish a strong digital presence because of limited resources, insufficient technical knowledge, or the cost of developing and maintaining independent digital platforms.

IFC BIZGROWTH aims to reduce these barriers by providing a centralized platform where businesses can create profiles, display their products and services, share business information, and explore opportunities to increase their visibility.

The platform combines business discovery, business profiles, promotional tools, advertising capabilities, marketing-service workflows, and business analytics within one digital ecosystem.

Rather than focusing exclusively on large companies, IFC BIZGROWTH is designed to create opportunities for small businesses, growing enterprises, independent entrepreneurs, service providers, and other legitimate businesses.

Our goal is to make digital business visibility more accessible while creating opportunities for businesses to grow beyond their immediate geographical communities.

Our Mission

To empower African businesses with accessible digital tools that improve online visibility, simplify business discovery, and create opportunities for sustainable growth.

Our Vision

To build a trusted, scalable African business-growth ecosystem that connects businesses, customers, entrepreneurs, and opportunities across Africa.

We envision a future where businesses can establish a digital presence, reach customers beyond their immediate communities, and participate more effectively in the digital economy.

The Problem We Address

Businesses can struggle with several challenges when trying to grow online:

- Limited visibility: Potential customers may not know that a business exists.
- Fragmented information: Business details, contact information, products, and services may be scattered across different platforms.
- High development costs: Not every business can afford a dedicated website, application, or extensive digital marketing campaign.
- Customer discovery challenges: Customers may find it difficult to discover suitable businesses and compare available offerings.
- Limited promotional opportunities: Small businesses may lack access to structured digital advertising and promotional tools.
- Difficulty measuring engagement: Business owners may have limited insight into how people interact with their online presence.
- Digital participation barriers: Some entrepreneurs need a simpler way to begin presenting their businesses online.

IFC BIZGROWTH aims to address these challenges through a centralized digital platform that brings business information, discovery, promotion, and growth-related tools together.

Our Solution

IFC BIZGROWTH provides a digital environment where businesses can:

1. Register an account and establish their business presence.
2. Create and manage a business profile.
3. Showcase products and services.
4. Present relevant business information, including contact details and location.
5. Provide customers with ways to contact or discover them.
6. Explore business promotion and advertising opportunities.
7. Request available marketing services.
8. Access business engagement information through supported analytics features.
9. Manage relevant business activities through the platform's business interface.

The platform is designed to support businesses throughout their digital visibility journey, from establishing an online presence to exploring promotional opportunities.

Free Services

IFC BIZGROWTH provides free entry-level business services to help entrepreneurs establish a digital presence and make their businesses discoverable.

1. Free Business Registration

Eligible businesses can create an account without a registration fee, making it easier to begin establishing their presence on the platform.

2. Business Profile Creation

Businesses can create profiles containing relevant information about their operations, including:

- Business name.
- Business description.
- Business category.
- Contact information.
- Business location.
- Operating hours.
- Social media links.
- Business images and other supported media.

These profiles provide a central place for presenting a business to potential customers.

3. Product Listings

Businesses can showcase their products through supported listings containing relevant descriptions, images, and other product information.

This helps potential customers explore what a business offers before making contact.

4. Service Listings

Service providers can present their services through their business profiles, helping customers discover businesses that may meet their needs.

5. Public Business Pages

Businesses can have public-facing pages that present their information and supported offerings.

These pages provide an accessible online destination where potential customers can learn about a business and its activities.

6. Customer Contact Opportunities

Public business profiles can provide supported ways for customers to connect with businesses, including:

- Telephone contact.
- Email contact.
- WhatsApp.
- Social media.
- Business location information.
- Other supported contact methods.

Available contact options depend on the information supplied by each business.

7. Business Discovery

Customers can explore participating businesses and their offerings through the platform's business discovery functionality.

This creates an additional opportunity for businesses to be found by potential customers.

8. Business Account Management

Registered businesses can access supported account-management features for managing their accounts and relevant business information.

Note: A free listing does not guarantee customer traffic, sales, search-engine rankings, or business growth.

Core Platform Features

1. Business Registration and Authentication

The platform supports business account creation and authentication workflows, including:

- Account registration.
- Login.
- Email one-time password (OTP) verification.
- Password recovery.
- Password reset.
- OTP verification and resend workflows.
- Account security and settings.

2. Business Profile Management

The business profile system supports the presentation and organization of information such as:

- Business details.
- Products and services.
- Media.
- Locations.
- Operating hours.
- Social links.
- Reviews.
- Verification information.

3. Product and Service Management

Businesses can manage supported product and service information associated with their profiles, allowing customers to explore individual offerings.

4. Business Verification

The platform includes business-verification functionality to support the review of submitted verification information.

Verification status is distinct from ordinary registration status. A business should only be described as verified after completing the applicable verification process.

Verification does not guarantee product quality, financial reliability, or future business performance.

5. Advertising Management

The advertising system includes functionality associated with:

- Advertising campaigns.
- Advertising creatives.
- Advertising packages.
- Advertising placements.
- Daily campaign statistics.
- Advertising events.

These components support the management and measurement of advertising activities.

6. Marketing-Service Requests

The platform includes functionality for organizing marketing-service requests, plans, and services.

This supports the management of requests and the services offered through the platform.

7. Business Engagement Analytics

The platform includes analytics functionality for supported business-profile and promotional interactions.

Tracked interactions may include:

- Business profile views.
- Product views.
- Service views.
- Promotion views.
- Website clicks.
- Telephone clicks.
- Contact clicks.
- WhatsApp clicks.
- Social media clicks.
- Location clicks.

These events help measure engagement with supported business information and promotional content.

Analytics should not be confused with guaranteed sales or independently verified advertising conversions.

8. Payment Processing

The platform includes payment-related functionality for supported services.

Payment-related components include payment orders, payment records, webhook events, and payment-processing workflows.

Paystack supports the platform's configured payment operations.

9. Localized Pricing

The backend includes functionality for localized pricing information, providing a foundation for presenting relevant payment information according to supported regional or currency settings.

10. Blog and Informational Content

The platform includes blog-related functionality intended to support informational content, business education, and platform updates when the relevant publishing features are available.

11. Administrative Management

The platform is being developed with a role-based administrative system intended to separate responsibilities and control access to relevant operational functions.

The intended administrative roles include:

- Super administrator.
- Business administrator.
- Advertising administrator.
- Finance administrator.
- Marketing administrator.
- Support administrator.
- Moderator.

Administrative capabilities depend on the implementation and deployment status of the admin panel.

How the Platform Works

Step 1: Create an Account

A business owner registers for an account and completes the required authentication process.

Step 2: Establish a Business Profile

The business supplies the relevant information needed to present itself on the platform.

Step 3: Add Products or Services

The business adds supported listings so potential customers can understand its offerings.

Step 4: Make the Business Discoverable

The public business profile allows customers to explore the business's information and available offerings.

Step 5: Connect With Potential Customers

Customers can use the contact methods provided by the business to make inquiries or request further information.

Step 6: Explore Promotional Opportunities

Businesses can explore available advertising packages or marketing services when these meet their needs.

Step 7: Review Engagement Information

Where analytics are available, businesses or authorized administrators can review supported engagement statistics.
